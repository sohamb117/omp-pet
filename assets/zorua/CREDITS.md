# Zorua sprite pack

Zorua is Pokemon #570. Sprite sheets and animation metadata downloaded from [PMDCollab/SpriteCollab](https://github.com/PMDCollab/SpriteCollab/tree/master/sprite/0570). These are community Pokemon Mystery Dungeon style sprites.

The upstream authors and asset-specific license identifiers are preserved verbatim in `upstream-credits.txt`; this includes contributors identified by Discord IDs 356635814668664832 and 544245909639397378. Pokemon characters belong to their respective owners. The app code license does not change the sprite rights.

The PNG sheets are unchanged. `manifest.json` selects the down-left direction and preserves the upstream durations (with an 80 ms minimum). Idle, walking, looking up, charging, hurt, sleeping, and hopping map to the companion's states.
